# Learning curriculum — editorial preview

60 micro-lessons, 300 lesson questions, 15 dedicated grammar drills, 60 vocabulary entries. Not all CEFR descriptors are covered; C1/C2 mastery requires expanded content and human assessment. All new lessons require mentor approval before official assessment. Each lesson has an objective, concept, vocabulary with meaning/example, correct and incorrect model, dialogue, device audio, interactive understanding and sentence practice, speaking case/rubric, quiz/explanation, error review and an objective checkpoint.

## Provenance and validation

BEDROOM VOCAB and the draft Workplace Communication syllabus were accessed through connected Drive on 26 November 2026. The syllabus supports workplace/email/telephone/presentation/discussion themes; it does not establish that all legacy questions were directly extracted or approved. Searches did not establish the original Worksheet Grammar, Phrases, Tongue Twisters or English Day source files. The E-Course folder was found but its full learning contents were not audited.

Technical reference registry: WCO Glossary (June 2024), WCO Harmonized System, WTO valuation, and the DJBC portal. Examples are original learning sentences; no customs duty rates, eligibility guarantees or current legal thresholds are supplied. Indonesian glosses are learning aids, not certified translations. A mentor must verify technical terminology and current official procedure before publication.

Recommended initial plan: 15 minutes, five days per week, one topic and due reviews. Placement selects a starting world and weak-skill priorities; estimation is internal only. Adaptive recommendations prioritise latest incorrect evidence, slow responses and repeated attempts, then skill focus and unfinished lessons. Official scores never derive from login or XP.
